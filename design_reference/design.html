Simulate hyperfine patterns and powder EPR spectra of paramagnetic transition metal complexes. The calculation includes g-factors (from d-orbital configurations), hyperfine coupling A (metal isotopes and ligands), and line shape profiles with optional tumbling. Select your metal and ligands, tweak the parameters — the spectrum updates in real time.
